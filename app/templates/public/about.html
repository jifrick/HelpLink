About HelpLink

HelpLink is a public-interest web platform built to solve information fragmentation. Everyday, thousands of valuable scholarships, entry-level jobs, internships, free learning courses, and community relief initiatives are posted across social media channels, messaging groups, and isolated websites.

Our mission is to create a single, clean, searchable community index where anyone can open HelpLink and answer: "What useful opportunity or assistance is available to me right now?"

Core Principles

  • Discovery First: No complex onboarding required to find value. Browse and search instantly.
  • Community Moderation: All submissions undergo review before publication to filter out spam and dead links.
  • Open Accessibility: Fast, mobile-first, zero annoying ads or paywalls.
